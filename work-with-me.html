// 01 · working session

A short, free look at your close

Your systems, your close calendar, where the manual work lives. You leave with an honest read on what's automatable and what it's worth — whether or not we work together.

Transactions coded at the source

The stack goes in — or your current one gets tuned. Bank feeds and rules, spend and receipt capture, AR and payments. The ledger largely writes itself; exceptions get reviewed instead of keyed.
