About this model
This assessment synthesizes published finance AI-maturity frameworks — Gartner's Finance AI Maturity Model, the FP&A Trends Group's Stages of AI Maturity in Finance, Databricks' AI Governance Maturity Model, and public AI-readiness scorecards — into ten finance-specific dimensions, each rated on a common five-phase scale: Exploring → Experimenting → Adopting → Scaling → Transforming. It's a conversation starter and a planning aid, not a certification.
