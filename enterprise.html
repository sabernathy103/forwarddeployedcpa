the idea

Your analysts already know what to build.
They've just never been allowed to ship.

Forward-deployed finance flips the usual IT project on its head. Instead of finance writing requirements and waiting in the backlog, the tools get built inside the finance team — with AI doing the coding, governed semantic models supplying one version of the numbers, and Entra ID governing who sees what. No shadow IT, no ungoverned spreadsheets: the platform is the guardrail, so the speed doesn't cost you control.
